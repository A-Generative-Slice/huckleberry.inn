Store Policies & Privacy

Clear guidelines for bespoke cake reservations, direct UPI verification, Chennai temperature-controlled transit, refunds, and your personal data.

1. Booking, 50% UPI Advance & Slot Confirmation

Every cake, hamper, and dessert at Your Huckleberry is handcrafted fresh from scratch in our 100% Pure Veg Eggless Nungambakkam kitchen. To confirm a baking slot, customers complete a 50% advance UPI payment (or full payment for ₹39 Trial / 7:00 PM Zero-Waste Saver items) to our verified merchant UPI ID (eoteatop@okicici) and upload their transfer screenshot during checkout. Baking begins once our kitchen verifies the transfer.

2. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

  • 48+ hours before delivery: Full refund of advance deposit or complimentary date reschedule.
  • 24 to 48 hours before delivery: 100% store credit valid for 60 days, or free reschedule subject to oven availability.
  • Under 24 hours / Same-day perishable orders: Ingredients, Belgian ganache, and fresh sponge prep have already started; therefore cancellations are non-refundable.
  • Quality / Transit Guarantee: If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, share a photo on WhatsApp (+91 85118 39668) within 2 hours of receipt for an immediate replacement or full refund.

3. Promotional Offers (TASTE39 & 7:00 PM Zero-Waste Saver)

  • TASTE39 Trial Hook: Strictly limited to 1 redemption per first-time Chennai buyer (phone/email). Valid for studio pickup in Nungambakkam or standard Chennai delivery zone fee.
  • 7:00 PM – 7:30 PM Zero-Waste Evening Saver: Daily surplus quantities are limited and sold on a first-verified basis for 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM central Chennai delivery or pickup.

4. Privacy Policy & Customer Data Protection

We collect only the information needed to bake, verify payment, and deliver your order: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, Chennai delivery address/pincode, and uploaded UPI transfer screenshot. Payment screenshots and customer contact details are stored securely in encrypted access-controlled storage accessible strictly to authorized Your Huckleberry kitchen staff. We never sell or rent your data to third parties. To request deletion of your contact details, email orders@yourhuckleberry.in or WhatsApp +91 85118 39668.
