Scheduled Time Windows

Air-Conditioned Transit Advisory

When picking up delicate multi-tiered or Lambeth piped cakes in Chennai, we strictly advise transporting the cake on a flat, level surface (such as the car footwell) with full vehicle air conditioning active.
