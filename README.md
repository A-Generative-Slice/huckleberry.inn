## ✨ Recent UX & Design Modernization

1. **Modern Mobile Navigation Drawer**:
   - Upgraded from translucent overlay to a solid opaque luxury drawer (`#FFFFFF`), completely preventing underlying page text from bleeding through.
   - Added a soft dark blurred backdrop (`#mobile-nav-backdrop`) that dims background content when open and dismisses on tap or `Escape`.
   - Redesigned each menu item into an accessible card with clear icons (🏠, 🍰, 🎂, 📦, 🖼️, 📍, ❓) and descriptive subtitles.
   - Embedded direct 1-tap WhatsApp kitchen contact button with real-time response indicator.

2. **Everyday-Friendly Selector Wording**:
   - Replaced overly technical and pretentious phrasing with clear, accessible terms for everyday customers:
     - *Scale & Form Factor* ➔ **Choose Cake Size** (Mini Box 4 Pcs, Bento 0.5 kg, Single Tier 1.0 kg, Two-Tier 2.5 kg, Three-Tier 4.5 kg).
     - *Artisan Flavor Palette* ➔ **Choose Cake Flavor** (Belgian Chocolate Truffle, Dark Chocolate & Salted Caramel, Lotus Biscoff, etc.).
     - *Hand-Piped Pâtisserie Finish* ➔ **Choose Cake Design Style** (Vintage Lambeth Ruffles, Rose Petal Swirls, Dark Velvet & Edible Gold, Berry Glaze Drip).
     - *Calligraphed Plaque* ➔ **Message on Cake** (Hand-piped lettering up to 26 characters).
     - *Chennai Delivery Zone & Oven Slot* ➔ **Delivery or Kitchen Pickup** (Simplified location names and readable time windows).

3. **Certified 100% Eggless Pure Veg Guarantee Card**:
   - Replaced awkward toggle switch and duplicate pills with a certified luxury trust card featuring the official FSSAI Pure Veg green emblem, verified check badge, and clear provenance copy.

4. **Image Tag Visibility Fix**:
   - Fixed mobile clipping on `.couture-tag-badge` by enforcing `transform: none !important;` and `left: 12px; top: 12px;`, ensuring tags like `10% OFF` are 100% visible and never cut in half by card overflow boundaries.

5. **Bracket Removal Across Entire Site**:
   - Removed all distracting square brackets `[]` from weights and badges (e.g., converted `[1 pc [160g]]` to `1 pc • 160g`, and `Quad Box [600g]` to `Quad Box (600g)`).
