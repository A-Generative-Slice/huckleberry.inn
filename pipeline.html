How Your Order Moves

4 Simple Kitchen Stages

Every order in our Nungambakkam scratch kitchen follows four transparent steps so you always know what's happening.

01

Payment Verified

You scan our official UPI QR (eoteatop@okicici) and upload your screenshot. Our kitchen confirms the transfer and locks your slot immediately.

02

Baked Fresh & Hand-Piped

100% Pure Veg Eggless sponge baked from scratch with Belgian Callebaut chocolate and French butter—never premixes or frozen stock.

03

Out for AC Delivery

Packed inside temperature-protected ribboned boxes and dispatched via air-conditioned Chennai transit (or ready for free studio pickup).

04

Delivered to Your Door

Arrives chilled and centerpiece-ready. Keep refrigerated at 4°C–8°C and rest 15 minutes before slicing for peak flavor.
